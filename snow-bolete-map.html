How to load many observations with an iNaturalist export

The quick loader above reads up to about 8,000 observations. For more, or to pick exact columns, use an export:

  1. Sign in at inaturalist.org (exports need an account).
  2. Open the export page. It should open with Boletus rex-veris and your dates filled in; if the filters are empty, set them yourself (species, date range, optional place).
  3. In the columns step, tick at least: id, observed_on, latitude, longitude. Also useful: place_guess, url, image_url, coordinates_obscured.
  4. Create the export and wait until it says it is ready (large ones can take several minutes), then download the .zip. You do not need to unzip it.
  5. Click Load export file above and choose the zip (or the CSV inside). The date range switches to the dates in the file.

Locations iNaturalist hides (obscured or private) are only precise to roughly 20 km, so they draw as hollow dots and can be switched off above.

About the snow layer

Modeled snow depth from NOAA NOHRSC (05 UTC daily, ~1 km grid, lower 48), taken from NOHRSC's georeferenced Google Earth tiles. Tiles load for the part of the map in view. Pixels with no snow are transparent.
